This portfolio states that development is AI-assisted, on the page, in Saathvik's own words.

This is not a disclaimer bolted on. It is the positioning. Saathvik's own instruction
from Intake Round 5:

> *"I do not want 'used a technology in an AI-assisted project' to automatically be
> treated as evidence of deep expertise. The final skill tiers should be based on what
> I can actually explain and debug."*

Every downstream phase inherits three rules from this:

1. **No skill tier claims debugging ability** until a specific, named instance exists.
2. **No project claims depth** its case study cannot defend under questioning.
3. **No number appears** that Saathvik cannot produce evidence for on request.
