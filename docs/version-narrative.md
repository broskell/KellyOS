# KELL.OS Version Narrative

**Status:** Confirmed by Saathvik in Intake Round 5, with the 1.0 end-date correction
applied (era 1.0 runs to **August 2025**, not June 2025 — the June–August 2025 prep gap
belongs to 1.0, since IIT Jodhpur began September 2025).

ceremony exists for returning visitors only.

## The three eras

Each KELL.OS version corresponds to a real period in Saathvik's life. There is no
fictional arc. Every claim below traces to an intake answer.

---

### KELL.OS 1.0 — "ORIGIN"

**Era:** 2023 – August 2025

**What defined it**
Intermediate (MPC), and technology as something adjacent to school rather than a
practice. Paid data-cleaning work on Fiverr in 2024 — three clients, roughly five to six
orders, approximately ₹30,000 total, cleaning and reformatting PDFs and Excel exports
with duplicate rows and spacing problems. No real software engineering. In Saathvik's own
words, this was the period of *watching YouTube videos for shortcuts and replicating
things to get output fast.*

Ends with the June–August 2025 gap: a self-funded Udemy Python course, Google
certifications, workshops, and the first deliberate move toward AI and Data Science.

**What ended it**
Entering IIT Jodhpur, September 2025.

**The honest reading:** money was earned, but nothing was built.

### KELL.OS 2.0

**Era:** September 2025 – May 2026

**What defined it**
Two programmes in parallel — the BS in Applied AI & Data Science at **IIT Jodhpur**, and
**LeapStart School of Technology**.

IIT Jodhpur delivered the foundations: Python, SQL, data analysis, NumPy/Pandas/
Matplotlib/scikit-learn, then Pattern Recognition (classification, CNNs, RNNs), AI
foundations (BFS, DFS, A\*, Minimax, Nash equilibrium), optimisation (gradient descent,
Newton's method, convex optimisation), and visualisation across Matplotlib, Plotly,
Tableau and Power BI.

LST delivered the stack: HTML/CSS/JavaScript and the DOM, then React, React Router,
Axios, Hooks, React Hook Form and Zod; PostgreSQL, PHP, REST APIs and Postman; Linux
administration and networking — SSH, tmux, disk management, port forwarding, tunnelling,
public/private IPs; Azure and Ubuntu VMs.

Alongside: the December 2025 SDLC workshop (business cases, project charters, FRDs) and
the Rapid Blood Bank Supply Platform MVP; cybersecurity tasks through Future Interns
(OWASP Juice Shop assessment with Burp Suite and Kali Linux, and a Python secure
file-sharing system); and PawSethu, built during the ProdX Buildathon.

Result: **SGPA 9.75** and **9.25**, **CGPA 9.44**.

**What ended it**
Completing Semester 2, and the shift from taught learning to independent work.

**The honest reading:** the volume era. Enormous breadth acquired, most of it taught
rather than earned through failure.

   building software" is the point of the era. Any later phase tempted to make 1.0 look
   like an engineering origin story is violating the honesty stance in
